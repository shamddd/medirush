I am pleased to submit our original research manuscript titled "MediRush-SafeAgent: A Policy-Constrained Multi-Stage Defense Framework for Reliable Healthcare-Commerce LLM Tool Execution" for publication as a regular research article in Artificial Intelligence in Medicine.

Large Language Model (LLM) agents are rapidly transitioning from text generation to active execution via external tool interfaces. When applied to digital healthcare-commerce—such as medicine search, inventory checking, and order processing—unconstrained tool calling introduces critical risks, including unauthorized prescription additions, cross-user data exposure, and indirect prompt injection attacks. 

In this work, we present MediRush-SafeAgent, a formal four-stage runtime defense framework that enforces pre-execution domain policy rules, scope authorization boundaries, post-execution environment state verification, and dynamic risk-adaptive human-in-the-loop escalation. We also introduce MediRushBench, a standardized benchmark comprising 24 scenario cases across 12 operational and security categories. Our empirical results demonstrate that MediRush-SafeAgent achieves a 100.0% Safe Task Completion Rate (STCR) and 0.0% Policy Violation Rate (PVR), outperforming unconstrained LLM baselines (STCR = 83.33%) and static prompt-guardrail baselines (STCR = 95.83%).

This manuscript has not been published previously, is not under consideration for publication elsewhere, and its publication has been approved by the author. I confirm that all research protocols, software baselines, and evaluation datasets are fully open-sourced for peer-review reproduction at https://github.com/shamddd/medirush.
