## Dual-Submission & Preprint Rules

- **Preprint Policy**: AAAI, AAMAS, NeurIPS, and USENIX allow posting non-anonymous preprints on arXiv prior to submission, provided the manuscript submitted to the venue remains strictly anonymized.
- **Dual Submission Rule**: Simultaneous submission of identical core contributions to multiple peer-reviewed conferences is strictly prohibited. Submissions will follow a sequential submission pipeline.
